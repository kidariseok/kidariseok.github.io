### **Subtopic 2: Types and Impacts of Infringements on Teachers' Rights**

**Personal Factors**
- **Emotional Stress**
  - **Emotional Issues of Teachers**
    - Increase in **Depression** and **Stress** Cases Due to Infringements on Teachers' Rights
    - Rise in Suicide Rates Among Teachers and Psychological Burdens
    - Cases Where Students and Parents Use Devices like Button Cameras and Smartwatches to Monitor Teachers, Increasing Psychological Stress and Distrust Among Teachers, Deepening Conflicts in the Educational Field. The Korean Teachers and Education Workers Union is advocating for legislation to prohibit unauthorized recordings, seeking legal measures to resolve related issues. This has become a major topic of discussion in social debates related to protecting teachers' rights and will be a key issue in the upcoming Stop Public Education Day protests.
- **Deterioration of Educational Environment**
  - Teachers Avoiding Duties Due to Excessive Demands from Students and Parents
  - Negative Impact on Students: Decline in the Quality of Education (Assuming Knowledge Already Acquired in Private Academies) and Loss of Educational Objectives → Infringements on Teachers' Rights Leading to Infringements on Students' Right to Learn
  - **Statistical Data**: Increase in Teacher Resignation and Sick Leave Rates, Decrease in Teacher Satisfaction
  - Survey Results on Teacher Job Satisfaction Showing the Highest Discontent Since 2006
- **Physical Stress**
  - **Cases of Physical Violence**
    - Incidents of Male High School Students Hitting Female Teachers; More Common in High Schools but Surprisingly Also Present in Middle and Elementary Schools
  - **Harassment and Sexual Assault Cases**
  - Cases of High School Seniors Sexually Harassing Teachers During Teacher Evaluations; Verbal Abuse and Harassment by Elementary School Students Towards Teachers

**Social Factors**
- **Work-Related Stress**
  - Parents demanding more attention for their child during field trips, threatening accusations of child neglect if their demands are not met. The effect is that teachers are reluctant to organize field trips, depriving students of valuable experiences.
  - Cases where students do not pay attention in class, claiming to have already learned the material in private academies, leading to a decline in the quality of education.

### **Subtopic 3: Stop Public Education Day**
- **Date of the Event**
  : Held on September 4, 2023, to commemorate the 49th day after the death of a teacher from Seoi Elementary School in Seocho District, Seoul.
- **Specific Events**
  : Nationwide teachers used collective leave and discretionary days off to gather in front of the National Assembly Building in Yeouido for a large-scale rally.
  : The rally included tributes to the deceased teacher and speeches demanding an investigation into the Seoi Elementary incident and addressing the hardships of teachers.
  : Some parents and students also participated, discussing issues across the education sector.
- **Demands Made**
  - Amendment of the Child Welfare Act
  - Ensuring the Educational Rights of Separated Students
  - Redefinition of School Violence
  - Protection of Teachers' Educational Rights
  - Establishment of a Complaint Handling System
  - Transfer of Childcare Responsibilities to Local Governments
  - Participation of Teachers in Policy Making
  - Enhancement of Accountability

  : Restoration of Teachers' Rights (Ensuring Legitimate Rights of Teachers to Prevent Unfair Disciplinary Actions by Parents or Administrators)
  : Legislative Reforms (Passage of Laws Related to Protection of Teachers' Rights)
  : Policy Improvements (Institutionalization for Teachers' Participation in Policy Making)
  : Complaint Handling System Improvements (Nationwide Standardization and Involvement of Teachers in the Establishment Process)
